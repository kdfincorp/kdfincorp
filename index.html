A few useful answers

Know what to expect.

What is Ultimate Business Software (UBS)?

Ultimate Business Software, or UBS, is the bookkeeping and billing application from KD SOFTWARES for Windows and Android. It brings company accounts, invoices, financial statements and GST reports into one installed application, with records saved locally on your device.

Do I need the internet to do my accounting?

Your books and accounting work stay on your device. An internet connection is needed for account verification, online activation and cloud backup or synchronisation.

How do I activate UBS?

Create an account and verify your email to start the 15-day trial. To continue with a paid licence, contact KD SOFTWARES. We approve the duration and the devices covered: Windows, Android or both.

How do backups and synchronisation work?

Eligible paid devices on the same account can check for newer cloud books when opening and ask to back up and sync on normal close. If both devices changed their books offline, you choose which complete copy to keep. Entries from two different copies are not merged.

Can I update without losing my books?

Keep a separate backup first, then install over the existing app. Do not uninstall Android UBS or clear its app storage. On Android, use Back or Close app to see the backup prompt; swiping the app away cannot guarantee an upload.
