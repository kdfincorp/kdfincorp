FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Product and account information.

What is Ultimate Business Software (UBS)?

Ultimate Business Software (UBS) is the accounting, invoicing and inventory application from KD FINCORP. Single PC and LAN plans support Windows, with Android companion access available for approved mobile accounts.

Do I need the internet to do my accounting?

An active Windows licence allows local accounting without a continuous internet connection. Internet access is required at least once every seven days to verify your licence, and for online activation, cloud backup and software updates. LAN users also need a connection to their office network.

How do I activate UBS?

Eligible new customers can start a 15-day free trial by creating a UBS account and verifying their email. Contact KD FINCORP to activate Single PC or UBS LAN and arrange setup.

Are backups included?

Cloud backup is included with your annual plan, within the backup-space allowance. UBS also lets you keep a separate local recovery copy. Our setup support helps you configure backups for your PC or office.

Can I update without losing my books?

Save a recovery backup and complete any unfinished entries before updating. Install over the existing application. On Android, keep the app installed and retain its storage to preserve saved books and account information.
