A few useful answers

Know what to expect.

What is Ultimate Business Software (UBS)?

Ultimate Business Software, or UBS, is the bookkeeping and billing application from KD FINCORP for Windows and Android. It brings company accounts, invoices, financial statements and GST reports into one installed application, with records saved locally on your device.

Do I need the internet to do my accounting?

Your books and accounting work stay on your device. An internet connection is needed for account verification, online activation and cloud backup or synchronisation.

How do I activate UBS?

Create an account and verify your email to start the 15-day free trial. To continue with a paid licence, contact KD FINCORP. Choose Single PC for one Windows PC or UBS LAN for your office team. Contact us for activation and setup.

Are backups included?

Cloud backup is included with your annual plan, within the backup-space allowance. UBS also lets you keep a separate local recovery copy. Our setup support helps you configure backups for your PC or office.

Can I update without losing my books?

Keep a separate backup first, then install over the existing app. Do not uninstall Android UBS or clear its app storage. On Android, use Back or Close app to see the backup prompt; swiping the app away cannot guarantee an upload.
