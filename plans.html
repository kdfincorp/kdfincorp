Backups and sync do different jobs

A recovery backup restores your books after a problem. Connected sends individual drafts to the main PC for posting; it does not replace whole databases between devices. Keep the main PC open and signed in to receive transactions. During the pilot, edit masters, make corrections, and run complete financial reports on the main PC. Review messages remain visible on the sending device.

Clear terms

  • 30-day first-device trial for newly eligible registrations. Trial restrictions and owner approval for extra devices still apply.
  • 1 GiB total retained cloud-backup allowance per business account, shared by current and Tuesday copies. Each archive must also fit the server archive limit. Cloud availability is capacity-controlled; the initial cohort is small. Local books remain on your PC.
  • Annual renewals use the published annual price. Prices shown exclude applicable taxes. Your written approval confirms the device allocation, term and any additional services before activation.
  • One standard remote setup session is included. Historical-data migration, customisation and additional training are quoted separately.
  • Existing licences are preserved. Customers are not moved to a new price or device allocation without explicit approval. New product plans require UBS 3.6.0 or later.
  • After expiry, saved local records remain yours. The account screen offers protected read-only invoice, ledger and journal views and CSV export. New postings and cloud services require an active subscription.
  • No lifetime cloud promise or unlimited storage. Keep a separate local recovery copy. Ordinary bug fixes do not carry a separate fee.
