3.2.3: Daily backups and update enrollment

  • Windows installers enroll daily complete local backups. Signed-in paid devices also make a daily cloud backup, due after 10 AM India time, with catch-up when the computer is available.
  • Each installed book folder has a separate daily cloud backup identity.
  • Android enrolls a persisted background job when opened, after an upgrade and after reboot. Android may defer jobs for battery management. Local backups work offline; cloud uploads wait for connectivity and an eligible account.
  • Windows and Android check the matching platform and edition for updates. Android verifies the existing package certificate and asks for normal system installation approval.
  • Validated recovery copies are kept before updates, restores and sync replacement. Daily retention settings do not delete recovery copies.
