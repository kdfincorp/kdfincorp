UBS release notes

3.2.2: Password visibility and searchable selections

  • Show/Hide controls for passwords, including backup protection, account access, staff login, company access, LAN and web forms. Passwords start hidden.
  • Dynamic case-insensitive, multiword filtering in selection lists, including accounts, items, currencies and tax options. Search text in a restricted field is not saved as a selection.

3.2.0 — 1 November 2026

Versioned Windows and Android packages include these changes. Check the official download filename and checksum to identify your installed package.

  • Configurable retention for automatic daily backups, with validation before older daily copies are removed.
  • Protected migration, update and sync recovery copies; atomic schema upgrades and recovery of the previous application runtime after a failed installer.
  • Automatic merging of new INR receipts/payments after devices share a baseline, with staff protection off. Staff-protected books require explicit conflict review. Existing edits/deletions and unsupported changes remain explicit conflicts. Repeated operations are deduplicated.
  • System Health checks and a sanitized support export.
  • Business intelligence and a local accounting-question model backed by the existing reports. It recognizes supported report questions and does not provide general-purpose chat or execute arbitrary SQL.
  • Local Windows image OCR and PDF/text extraction into unposted invoice drafts. Review the original and actual item lines before saving a purchase bill. Android supports PDF/text draft import.
  • Controlled multi-company reporting and a separate customer account portal.
  • Owner dashboard fields for reported app version, last connection, last successful book upload and recorded backup date.

Before upgrading, retain a validated recovery backup and its password. Keep existing Android storage when updating the app.
