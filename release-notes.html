UBS release notes

Product updates are listed below, with the most recent release first. Historical notes describe the features available in each release; current access and backup arrangements depend on your approved plan.

Current plans and pricing · Installation and support · Latest downloads

3.6.1 — Plans and 15-day free trial

  • Updated annual pricing and device allowances for Single PC, UBS LAN and the Connected pilot. See the plans page for current prices.
  • Set the default trial for eligible new registrations to 15 days. Previously approved trial periods and signed licences retain their agreed terms.
  • Updated the website and activation tools to use the current plan details.

3.6.0 — Annual plans and Connected pilot

  • Introduced Single PC, office LAN and Connected annual plans with specified device and backup-storage allowances.
  • Introduced an invitation-only Connected pilot for new INR sales, receipts and payments, with posting confirmed by a designated Windows PC.
  • Improved backup permissions, interrupted-transfer recovery and duplicate prevention. Added protected viewing and export of saved local records after licence expiry.

Android 3.5.10 — Update verification

  • Improved update verification on Android 9 and certain emulators, including BlueStacks, when Android does not provide its newer signing information.
  • Retained checks for the application identity, signing certificate and update version.

3.5.9 — Backup confirmation and mobile tools

  • Improved confirmation of the saved backup when more than one backup operation completes at a similar time.
  • Expanded access to supported mobile invoice, payment, ledger, item, stock and reporting screens. Removed retired workflow links.

3.5.8 — Transfer progress

  • Added progress information for backup and synchronisation, including the current step, transferred data, speed and elapsed time.
  • Displayed time estimates where sufficient transfer or previous-check information was available. Retained recovery copies and conflict review.

Android 3.5.7 — Interrupted synchronisation recovery

  • Improved recovery after interrupted synchronisation and reused validated downloads where possible.
  • Added clearer interrupted-operation messages. Database checks and recovery operations still need to finish before the app is closed.

3.5.6 — Assistant controls and support details

  • Updated support contact details and refined the Windows assistant, searchable billing fields and editable delivery details.
  • Retained offline Android guidance and supported local reports. Processing large company databases may still take additional time.

3.5.5 — Billing and Android improvements

  • Improved party and item selection, live search and delivery-address editing in bill forms.
  • Corrected Android layouts and supporting backup components, and improved synchronisation recovery.

3.5.3 — Branding and contact information

  • Updated KD FINCORP branding, support details, platform icons and social links across the website and applications.
  • Retained the existing bill-scanning, reviewed-save and startup-update improvements.

Windows 3.5.2 — Bill scanning and startup updates

  • Improved offline extraction of invoice totals, tables, rotated images, product codes and rounded amounts. Extracted information still requires review.
  • Added clearer financial-year checks when saving reviewed bills and retained duplicate and total-mismatch checks.
  • Moved update checks into the background and improved interrupted-download recovery. Existing Windows AI models are retained during updates.

Windows 3.4.6 — Simplified sales navigation

  • Removed retired Quotation and Quick Billing menu entries. Sales billing is available through Transactions > Sales > Sales invoice.
  • Retained saved records, offline bill scanning and payment-allocation features.

Windows 3.4.5 — Bill review and payment allocation

  • Improved offline PDF and image bill review, including party details, product lines, tax values and rounded totals.
  • Added allocation of receipts and payments to unpaid bills, including partial payments and advances.
  • Expanded approved LAN and cloud-backup options. These historical features remain subject to the customer's approved plan.

3.4.1 — Shared installers and account-based activation

  • Introduced a shared installer for each platform, with approved account terms controlling devices and licence validity.
  • Improved document remarks, order conversion, invoice collections, credit controls and stock tools.
  • Removed experimental e-invoice and e-way bill screens. Standard invoice PDFs and GST reports remained available.

3.2.5 — Conversational accounting assistant

  • Introduced a persistent assistant chat that preserves the current entry form.
  • Added suggested journal entries, follow-up questions, invoice review and business summaries. Suggestions require review and do not post transactions automatically.
  • Improved Android chat layout, message entry and back navigation.

3.2.4 — Offline guidance and responsiveness

  • Expanded offline app guidance and supported accounting-report answers, with the relevant company and report identified.
  • Improved large selection lists, background startup checks and Android screen updates.

3.2.3 — Scheduled backups and protected updates

  • Added scheduled local backups and supported cloud-backup operations.
  • Added Android background scheduling, which remains subject to Android battery and background-execution settings.
  • Retained validated recovery copies before updates, restores and supported synchronisation operations.

3.2.2 — Password controls and search

  • Added password visibility controls across supported account, company, staff and backup forms. Passwords remain hidden by default.
  • Improved case-insensitive, multiword filtering in selection lists.

3.2.0 — Recovery and reporting improvements

  • Added configurable backup retention, protected recovery copies and safer update and database-upgrade handling.
  • Added System Health reporting, supported local accounting answers, invoice-draft extraction and customer-account tools.
  • Introduced controlled synchronisation for supported entries. Existing conflicts and unsupported changes required review.
