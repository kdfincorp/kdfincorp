UBS release notes

Windows 3.4.6 — simpler sales workflow

Quotation and Quick Billing screens are removed from the menus and screen search. Use Transactions → Sales → Sales invoice for billing. Offline bill scanning, voucher allocation and existing records are retained.

Windows 3.4.5 — offline bill scanning and smoother workflows

  • Read PDF and image bills offline using bundled OCR; review extracted items, parties, GST and totals before posting. Create missing masters from the review screen.
  • Better photographed-sheet correction, table columns, wrapped descriptions, spaced numbers, dates and signed round-off. Ambiguous product codes still need checking.
  • Receipt and payment vouchers can allocate amounts to unpaid bills, including partial payments and advances.
  • Integrated LAN for admin-approved licences, opt-in live Windows cloud backup and a round movable penguin assistant.
  • Cloud retention: current backup and the last two Tuesday snapshots when available. Uploads require the PC to be running and connected.

Android remains on 3.4.1. Update Windows in place and keep a separate recovery backup.

3.4.1 — shared apps and simpler workflows

  • One Windows installer and one Android APK for every customer plan. Signed admin approval controls activation, devices and validity.
  • Approved plan upgrades apply through licence sync without reinstalling the app.
  • Remarks save, reopen and print on sales/purchase bills, orders and credit/debit notes. Order conversion preserves remarks.
  • Invoice collections, partial payments/refunds, credit limits, quotations, Quick Billing, receipt previews, stock alerts and batch/serial/expiry tracking.
  • Home, Refresh and Find Screen remain on the right, with visible form actions and smoother scrolling.
  • The e-invoice/e-way sandbox has been removed. Ordinary invoice PDFs and GST reports remain available.

3.2.5: One conversational UBS Assistant

  • A persistent Ask UBS corner button opens a single chat without replacing the current entry form. The former Business Intelligence, Accounting Assistant and Smart Document Entry menu entries now lead to one UBS Assistant.
  • Explain a transaction to receive proposed debit/credit lines, an explanation and narration. Follow-up questions clarify payment method, loan interest, inventory policy and tax components instead of silently assuming them.
  • Invoice reading and business overview are available in the same chat. Suggestions and invoice drafts do not post to books. Company or user changes clear the chat context.
  • Android has a dedicated conversation area and pinned message box, with modal protection, minimization and Back navigation verified.

3.2.4: Offline accounting assistant and responsiveness

  • Expanded bundled offline accounting-question model and app guidance for backups, sync, invoice drafts, ledgers, items and bank reconciliation. Common questions calculate only the relevant report. Answers retain their report sources and company scope.
  • Assistant guidance is available without an open company; invoice extraction remains local and creates a draft for review.
  • Large Windows selection lists show searchable batches with a Show more option, avoiding thousands of row insertions on every keystroke.
  • Local books open without waiting for the startup cloud check. Cloud differences are reported in the background and reviewed through Back up & synchronise.
  • Android skips serialization for already accepted, unchanged screens. Pending edits, notices and unaccepted screen updates are retained.

3.2.3: Daily backups and update enrollment

  • Windows installers enroll daily complete local backups. Signed-in paid devices also make a daily cloud backup, due after 10 AM India time, with catch-up when the computer is available.
  • Each installed book folder has a separate daily cloud backup identity.
  • Android enrolls a persisted background job when opened, after an upgrade and after reboot. Android may defer jobs for battery management. Local backups work offline; cloud uploads wait for connectivity and an eligible account.
  • Windows and Android check the matching platform and edition for updates. Android verifies the existing package certificate and asks for normal system installation approval.
  • Validated recovery copies are kept before updates, restores and sync replacement. Daily retention settings do not delete recovery copies.

3.2.2: Password visibility and searchable selections

  • Show/Hide controls for passwords, including backup protection, account access, staff login, company access, LAN and web forms. Passwords start hidden.
  • Dynamic case-insensitive, multiword filtering in selection lists, including accounts, items, currencies and tax options. Search text in a restricted field is not saved as a selection.

3.2.0 — 1 November 2026

Versioned Windows and Android packages include these changes. Check the official download filename and checksum to identify your installed package.

  • Configurable retention for automatic daily backups, with validation before older daily copies are removed.
  • Protected migration, update and sync recovery copies; atomic schema upgrades and recovery of the previous application runtime after a failed installer.
  • Automatic merging of new INR receipts/payments after devices share a baseline, with staff protection off. Staff-protected books require explicit conflict review. Existing edits/deletions and unsupported changes remain explicit conflicts. Repeated operations are deduplicated.
  • System Health checks and a sanitized support export.
  • Business intelligence and a local accounting-question model backed by the existing reports. It recognizes supported report questions and does not provide general-purpose chat or execute arbitrary SQL.
  • Local Windows image OCR and PDF/text extraction into unposted invoice drafts. Review the original and actual item lines before saving a purchase bill. Android supports PDF/text draft import.
  • Controlled multi-company reporting and a separate customer account portal.
  • Owner dashboard fields for reported app version, last connection, last successful book upload and recorded backup date.

Before upgrading, retain a validated recovery backup and its password. Keep existing Android storage when updating the app.
